Zofia Anna Olesik - Biography

A 20-year-old violinist from Silesia, Zofia Anna Olesik studies at the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Academy of Music in Poznań in the class of Prof. Marcin Baranowski. She has won prizes at more than 30 national and international competitions, including the Karol Lipiński and Henryk Wieniawski International Violin Competition in Lublin, the Jascha Heifetz Competition in Vilnius, the Aram Khachaturian Competition in Yerevan, the Arthur Grumiaux Competition in Brussels, the Michał Spisak Competition in Dąbrowa Górnicza, and the Zdzisław Jahnke Competition in Poznań.

She has received a Young Poland scholarship from the Polish Ministry of Culture and National Heritage, as well as scholarships from the Prime Minister of Poland, the cities of Poznań and Katowice, and the Marshal of the Greater Poland Voivodeship. She has also twice been awarded a scholarship to the prestigious Morningside Music Bridge program in the United States.

Despite her young age, she has already performed with many renowned orchestras, including the Lithuanian National Philharmonic, Sinfonia Iuventus, the Armenian State Symphony Orchestra, the Pomeranian Philharmonic, the Poznań Philharmonic, and the Oxford Philharmonic Orchestra. She has also performed at Buckingham Palace. She collaborated with the Vienna Philharmonic on the Penderecki Project and has worked with the European Union Youth Orchestra and the Oxford Philharmonic Orchestra. She has honed her skills under such distinguished artists as Julia Fischer, Midori, Vadim Gluzman, and Donald Weilerstein.

Zofia Anna Olesik was the first participant officially announced for the forthcoming 17th Henryk Wieniawski International Violin Competition.

Łukasz Byrdy - Biography

Łukasz Byrdy is one of the most distinctive pianists of the younger generation. A prizewinner at more than 20 international piano competitions, he has performed for over a decade on prestigious stages across Europe, the Americas, and Asia. Born in Katowice, he won top prizes at the Fryderyk Chopin International Piano Competition in Beijing and the International Piano Competition in Hanover, among others. He has also received awards in Switzerland, Italy, Malta, and Poland.

His concert career developed significantly following his participation in the 17th International Fryderyk Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw. In 2022, he was one of ten semifinalists at the prestigious Honens International Piano Competition in Calgary. He has received numerous scholarships and artistic awards, including the Young Poland scholarship and scholarships from the Polish Ministry of Culture and National Heritage.

He has appeared at many prestigious music festivals, including Chopin and His Europe, the International Paderewski Festival, Chopin in the Colours of Autumn, the Christopher Festival in Vilnius, the Silesian Quartet and Its Guests Chamber Music Festival in Katowice, the International Chopin Festival in Duszniki-Zdrój, the Jerzy Waldorff Festival in Radziejowice, Chopin au Jardin and Chopin à Paris at the Orangerie in Paris's Parc de Bagatelle, and the Aspen Music Festival and School in the United States. In Poland, he has performed with most of the country's philharmonic orchestras and in its leading concert halls, including the Polish National Radio Symphony Orchestra in Katowice, the Witold Lutosławski Concert Studio of Polish Radio in Warsaw, and the Krzysztof Penderecki European Centre for Music in Lusławice.

Łukasz Byrdy completed his piano studies at the Berlin University of the Arts and the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Academy of Music in Poznań. He is currently an assistant professor in the Piano Department at the Academy of Music in Poznań and at the Faculty of Pedagogy and Fine Arts of Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań. He is also an active teacher, gives numerous masterclasses, and collaborates with music academies in North America, China, and many European countries. He has repeatedly been invited to teach at the renowned Morningside Music Bridge program for young talents at the New England Conservatory of Music in Boston.

He has made numerous radio and television recordings broadcast in Poland, China, and Lithuania. His debut album, featuring piano concertos by Fryderyk Chopin and Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, was released in 2017.

Marcin Saja - Biography

Marcin Saja, born in 2007, is currently a piano student at the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Academy of Music in Poznań, where he studies with Prof. Katarzyna Popowa-Zydroń and Dr Łukasz Byrdy. He graduated from the Fryderyk Chopin State Music School in Warsaw, where he studied piano with Prof. Grzegorz Mania.

He has won prizes at numerous national and international piano competitions, including the Premio Scarlatti International Piano Competition in Vilnius, the Béla Bartók International Piano Competition in Warsaw, and the Juliusz Zarębski International Music Competition in Warsaw. He was also a finalist at the Jeune Chopin International Piano Competition in Lugano.

He has performed at Fryderyk Chopin's Birthplace in Żelazowa Wola, the Witold Lutosławski Concert Studio of Polish Radio in Warsaw, the Fryderyk Chopin University of Music in Warsaw, the Gardzienice Theatre, and Łańcut Castle. He also appeared in an anniversary concert for the Queen of Denmark, organized by the Embassy of the Kingdom of Denmark in Poland, alongside the Copenhagen Sankt Annæ Symphony Orchestra.

He has taken part in masterclasses led by distinguished pianists and teachers, including Andrzej Jasiński, Wojciech Świtała, and Philippe Giusiano.

Piotr Alexewicz - Biography

Piotr Alexewicz is one of the most recognizable Polish pianists of his generation. He won the fifth prize, the Audience Award, and several special prizes at the 19th International Fryderyk Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw.

In 2025, he won the second prize at the Hilton Head International Piano Competition in the United States, further consolidating his position on the international piano scene. In the same year, he was a semifinalist at the 17th Van Cliburn International Piano Competition in Fort Worth.

He received the Professor Zbigniew Drzewiecki Award at the 18th International Fryderyk Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw and twice won the 48th and 50th National Chopin Piano Competitions organized by the Fryderyk Chopin Institute. He has also won top prizes at piano competitions in Paris and Bucharest.

In 2024, he won the concerto competition at the PianoTexas International Festival & Academy, performing Johannes Brahms's Piano Concerto No. 1 in D minor, Op. 15, with the Fort Worth Symphony Orchestra under Miguel Harth-Bedoya. That same year, he became one of three laureates of the prestigious Schenk Competition in Switzerland and, for the first time in the competition's history, received every special prize, including the Audience Award and the Orchestra Award.

His extensive discography includes a debut album released by the Fryderyk Chopin Institute in 2019, featuring works by Beethoven, Chopin, Liszt, and Ravel. With Sinfonia Varsovia under Howard Shelley, he recorded all of Chopin's works for piano and orchestra other than the concertos. Three albums containing all of his solo performances at the 17th Van Cliburn International Piano Competition in Fort Worth were released in 2025.

He has appeared at prestigious international festivals, including Chopin and His Europe in Warsaw, the International Chopin Festival in Duszniki-Zdrój, the Chopin Festival in Nohant, Festival Chopin do Brasil, PianoTexas, the Arthur Rubinstein Festival in Łódź, the Chopin Festival in Connecticut, the Kawai Piano Series, the Gdańsk Music Festival, and recitals in Żelazowa Wola.

He has performed at renowned venues including the Berlin Philharmonie, Salle Cortot in Paris, the National Forum of Music in Wrocław, the Warsaw Philharmonic, Adam Mickiewicz University Hall in Poznań, Teatro Nacional in Brasília, Sala São Paulo, Van Cliburn Concert Hall, Ed Landreth Auditorium, and Bass Performance Hall in Fort Worth, the Woodruff Arts Center in Atlanta, Sapporo Concert Hall Kitara, ACROS Fukuoka Concert Hall, Symphony Hall in Osaka, Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre, Musashino Bunka Kaikan, and Seoul Arts Center.

He has worked with distinguished conductors including Marek Pijarowski, Carlos Miguel Prieto, Andrey Boreyko, Manuel Hernández-Silva, Ariel Zuckermann, Benjamin Schwartz, Przemysław Neumann, Hansjörg Schellenberger, Marc Kissoczy, Daniel Stabrawa, Thomas Koncz, Rune Bergmann, Adam Banaszak, Nicolae Moldoveanu, Howard Shelley, Ruben Silva, Jakub Chrenowicz, Norton Morozowicz, and Tatsuya Shimono.

In recognition of his artistic achievements, First Lady of Poland Agata Kornhauser-Duda named him a Young Promoter of Poland in 2021 for his outstanding contribution to promoting Polish culture. That same year, he received the Wrocław Artistic Award. He also received the Lower Silesian Key to Success in 2025 and was named one of Wrocław's 30 Creative People in 2026.

He maintains an active international concert schedule, performing in Poland, Switzerland, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Romania, Denmark, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Japan, South Korea, Morocco, and many other countries. Since 2024, he has taught a piano class at the Karol Lipiński Academy of Music in Wrocław.

Piotr Alexewicz began his music studies with Paweł Zawadzki at the Karol Lipiński Academy of Music in Wrocław and now continues them with Konstantin Scherbakov at Zurich University of the Arts. He has also studied with Nikolai Demidenko.

Barbara Żołnierczyk - Biography

Barbara Żołnierczyk graduated from the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Academy of Music in Poznań, where she studied with Prof. Bartosz Bryła and Prof. Karina Gidaszewska.

From an early age, she competed in national and international violin competitions, winning top prizes and distinctions. She won her first competition at the age of eight, taking first place in the Janko Muzykant National Violin Competition in Sochaczew.

She has won many competitions, including first prizes at the 4th International Music Competition in Paris (2019), the 10th International Music Competition in Belgrade (2019), the 11th Stanisław Serwaczyński National Competition for Young Violinists in Lublin (2020), the 10th Zdzisław Jahnke National Violin Competition in Poznań (2020), and the 3rd and 4th Violin Talents National Competitions in Poznań (2019 and 2021). She also reached the semifinals of the televised Young Musician of the Year competition in 2018 and twice received the Young Poland scholarship, in 2014 and 2020.

During the 2021/2022 season, she appeared as a soloist with the AMADEUS Chamber Orchestra of Polish Radio and performed repeatedly with the Poznań Philharmonic Orchestra. In subsequent years, she also performed with the Koszalin Philharmonic Orchestra and the Sudeten Philharmonic Orchestra.

Barbara Żołnierczyk will represent Poland at the 17th Henryk Wieniawski International Violin Competition in Poznań in October 2026.

Maciej Kułakowski - Biography

Born in Gdańsk in 1996, Maciej Kułakowski began playing the cello at the age of six in the class of Prof. Jadwiga Ewald.

He is a laureate of the Queen Elisabeth Competition in Brussels and won both the first prize and a special prize at the 10th Witold Lutosławski International Cello Competition in Warsaw. He has been represented by London's Young Classical Artists Trust since 2019.

Highlights of this season include concerts at Wigmore Hall, the Concertgebouw Amsterdam, Pierre Boulez Saal, the Cologne Philharmonie, the Casals Forum in Kronberg, and Schloss Elmau. As a soloist, he will perform with the Poznań Philharmonic Orchestra, Olsztyn Philharmonic Orchestra, Opole Philharmonic Orchestra, Toruń Symphony Orchestra, and Polish Baltic Philharmonic.

In 2022, Delphian Records released his critically acclaimed debut album of French music, Beau soir. He subsequently recorded the complete works for solo cello by Krzysztof Penderecki; the recording was released by DUX in 2023.

He has performed throughout Europe and beyond with ensembles including the Warsaw Philharmonic Orchestra, Sinfonietta Cracovia, Polish Baltic Philharmonic Orchestra, London Mozart Players, Kammerphilharmonie Bremen, Brussels Philharmonic, St Petersburg Symphony Orchestra, and Calgary Philharmonic Orchestra. As a chamber musician, he has appeared at the Piatigorsky Cello Festival in Los Angeles, Kronberg Festival, Moritzburg Festival, Cello Biennale Amsterdam, Seiji Ozawa Matsumoto Festival in Japan, Krzysztof Penderecki Festival, Witold Lutosławski Festival, Munich Rising Stars, At the World's Edge in New Zealand, and Beijing Super Cello Festival.

He has collaborated across a broad repertoire with musicians including Krzysztof Penderecki, Mischa Maisky, Emanuel Ax, Christoph Eschenbach, Viviane Hagner, Giovanni Sollima, Charles Neidich, Arnold Steinhardt, Lawrence Power, and Kian Soltani, as well as conductors Eivind Gullberg Jensen, Frank Braley, Stéphane Denève, Marek Moś, and Paweł Przytocki. Maciej studied with Wolfgang Emanuel Schmidt at Kronberg Academy and the Hochschule für Musik in Weimar, and with Marcin Zdunik at the Stanisław Moniuszko Academy of Music in Gdańsk.

He plays a 1700 cello by Giovanni Battista Ruggeri and a modern cello made by Thorsten Theis in 2014.

Michał Francuz - Biography

Michał Francuz graduated from the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Academy of Music in Poznań, where he studied with Prof. Waldemar Andrzejewski. He now teaches in the academy's Piano Department and Department of Piano Chamber Music, where he also gives international masterclasses and lectures for young musicians.

A soloist, chamber musician, and teacher, he has been active on the international stage for more than 20 years. His concert career has taken him to leading venues across Europe, North and South America, Asia, and the Middle East. He has performed at New York's Carnegie Hall, where he made his debut in May 2015, at Fryderyk Chopin's Birthplace in Żelazowa Wola, in Warsaw's Royal Łazienki Park, and at events and festivals in Algeria, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, and Vietnam.

He has worked with leading Polish symphony and chamber orchestras, including the Poznań, Szczecin, Opole, and Kraków philharmonic orchestras, Sinfonia Varsovia, Baltic Neopolis Orchestra, FeelHarmony Orchestra, Collegium F Orchestra, and l'Autunno Chamber Orchestra. He has also appeared on opera and theatre stages, including the Grand Theatre in Poznań, the Polish Theatre and Chamber Theatre in Szczecin, and the Baltic Opera in Gdańsk. In 2016 and 2022, he served as an official pianist of the Henryk Wieniawski International Violin Competition in Poznań.

His concert and recording partners include Bartłomiej Nizioł, Iwona Sobotka, Seiji Okamoto, Richard Lin, the Silesian Quartet, Stefan Dohr, Agata Zubel, Jarosław Nadrzycki, Jakub Haufa, and Piotr Pławner.

He regularly appears at international music festivals, including Ming Classic, La Folle Journée Chopin - Chopin Open in Warsaw, the Cinquième Festival Culturel International de Musique Symphonique in Algeria, the Lubomirski Festival, Music on the Heights in Zakopane, Sacrum non Profanum in Trzęsacz, Szczecin Classic, the Ignacy Jan Paderewski Festival in Warsaw, and Chopin in the Colours of Autumn in Antonin.

His work also promotes contemporary music and Poland's compositional heritage. In 2019, he released the album Polish Violin Sonatas, recorded with Bartłomiej Nizioł. In 2020, he recorded albums with Joanna Konarzewska and Rafał Kwiatkowski, featuring piano trios by Władysław Żeleński and Grzegorz Fitelberg and the world premiere recording of a trio by Apolinary Szeluto, and with the Silesian Quartet, featuring Żeleński's piano trio and piano quartet. He has made numerous recordings for Polish Radio and Polish Television.

Aleksandra Martinoska - Biography

Aleksandra Martinoska was born in 1994. She graduated from the Zenon Brzewski General Secondary Music School in Warsaw, where she studied violin with Prof. Magdalena Szczepanowska. She continued her musical education in Vienna with Prof. Pavel Vernikov at the Music and Arts University of the City of Vienna, graduating in 2020. She subsequently completed postgraduate studies at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna in the classes of Prof. Lieke te Winkel and Prof. Michael Frischenschlager in 2026.

She has won prizes at numerous national and international competitions. Her most important achievements include fifth place at the 5th Tadeusz Wroński International Solo Violin Competition, first place at the Young Paganini International Violin Competition in Legnica, second place at the Jeunesses International Music Competition in Bucharest, fourth place at the International Violin Competition in Kloster Schöntal, Germany, second place at the 51st Kocian Violin Competition in the Czech Republic, and first place and the grand prize at the 12th Bohdan Warchal Talents for Europe International String Competition in Slovakia.

As a soloist, she has performed with Sinfonia Viva, Sinfonia Varsovia, the State Chamber Orchestra of Žilina, the Calgary Philharmonic Orchestra, and the Opole Philharmonic Orchestra. She has given concerts in Austria, Italy, Norway, Canada, and Slovakia. She has participated in numerous national and international masterclasses, studying with Prof. Wanda Wiłkomirska, Prof. Eugeny Durnovo, Prof. Zakhar Bron, Prof. Kaja Danczowska, Prof. Gwen Hoebig, Prof. Ian Swensen, Prof. Bill van der Sloot, Prof. Yuri Volguin, and Prof. Ning Feng.

Her artistic work has earned her numerous scholarships and distinctions, including a Young Poland scholarship, a scholarship from the Polish Ministry of Culture and National Heritage for the 2012/2013 academic year, and the Franciszek Wybrańczyk Scholarship for Outstanding Young Polish Musicians, funded by the Sinfonia Varsovia Foundation. From 2008 to 2013, she was also a scholarship holder of the Polish Children's Fund.

Since 2014, Aleksandra Martinoska has been a member of the first violin section of the Tonkünstler-Orchester Niederösterreich.

Filip Pinczewski - Biography

Filip Pinczewski is a pianist, composer, and teacher, born in Kalisz in 1993. He graduated from the Jadwiga Kaliszewska General Music School in Poznań, where he studied piano with Prof. Waldemar Andrzejewski, and from the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna, where he studied piano with Prof. Manfred Wagner-Artzt as well as composition and music theory.

As a pianist, he performs as a soloist and chamber musician, with a particular interest in contemporary music. He has given concerts and had his works performed in many European countries, including Poland, Austria, Germany, Italy, Norway, Iceland, Malta, Latvia, Hungary, and Switzerland. His performing activity combines the classical solo and chamber piano repertoire with contemporary music and includes world premieres. As a performer of contemporary music, he has appeared at the Wien Modern festival and the Mauricio Kagel International Composition Competition in Vienna.

He combines his artistic career with teaching and academic work. He is a piano teacher and accompanist at the Regionalmusikschule Kreuzenstein and a lecturer and doctoral candidate at the Department of Music Education Research and Practice at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna.

He is a co-founder of the Calisia Arts Foundation and co-initiator of the concert series Let's Open Up to New Music and Student Musical Quarters. A prizewinner at piano and composition competitions, he is also a two-time scholarship holder of the Piotr Kler Foundation, whose support enabled him to record a solo album devoted to the music of Johann Sebastian Bach.
